# v0.9 actual review and acceptance boundaries

## Artifact coverage

The public `examples/validated-v090/manifest.json` binds **803** complete rendered
pages to exact export hashes and an explicit screen-review ledger:

| Scope | Pages | Actual review |
|---|---:|---|
| Native source examples | 313 | All complete images screened; dense/changed examples enlarged |
| 87 drawing forms at small/medium/large data volumes | 261 | Every complete image screened; changed files rechecked |
| 42 distinct component relationships, three layouts | 146 | Every complete page and component identity screened; reading pagination retained |
| 19 fixed reference scenes/palettes, two widths | 38 | Complete framing only; source content unchanged |
| Coherent bulk and single-cell sets | 25 | Every complete single figure, main figure and guide screened |
| Explicit main-figure layouts | 8 | Four modes per assay, complete selected panels; all screened |
| Public penguin measurements and declared curve fixtures | 12 | Three data volumes and 1/5/9-model curve facets screened |

Counts describe pages, not distinct chart types, statistics or independent
scientific validations. Historical aliases are preserved. A source and a volume
fixture may legitimately render identical pixels when the original source has
only a few records; this is not evidence of a large-data stress test.

## Ordinary tasks and print scope

Six ordinary Chinese requests actually selected the QC, differential/pathway
and complete-manuscript sets for both assays. Bulk has 11 figures and single-cell
has 14 in the supplied fictional result package. Missing upstream channels are
not generated. The main figures present complementary channels rather than all
diagnostics in one small page. Selected single-cell donor effects explicitly
belong to the myeloid example. Illustrative values reused from the bulk fixture
are disclosed and do not constitute independent biological validation.

The two main PDFs were also raster-read at nominal **178 mm / 180 DPI**, with
essential declared text measuring about **7.69 pt** at that width. Companion
specifications use 210 mm and a 7 pt blocking threshold. The eight alternate
layouts use their explicitly recorded widths (178, 210 or 330 mm). All have
measured-font passes. A screen image scaled to nominal millimetres is not a
calibrated paper proof or perceptual verification of every printed font.

The general source library, volume checks and large historical compositions
remain **target-size unassessed**. Use complete single panels or the paginated
reading mode when a dense composition cannot fit a manuscript page. Retained
illustrative compound styles are not recommended as narrow-column main figures.
Do not turn a layout screen pass into a blanket publication-size approval.

## Repairs driven by viewing actual exports

- Measured caption and x-label lanes, complete legends and long axis labels.
- Exact matrix/count-track alignment with a physical gap after reflow.
- Multi-model curves split into complete facets, with a centred incomplete row.
- Long annular categories use an aligned linear extension with exact values.
- Faint flow-gate points retain their continuous values and get a neutral outline.
- Dense MA backgrounds remain visible behind the supplied significant results.
- A vertical single-cell main figure exposed a misplaced panel title; headings
  are now explicitly distinguished from footnotes, and the donor-composition
  label lane was enlarged. The repaired actual export and regression were checked.

## Evidence and limitations

The ledger records exact PNGs actually viewed or byte-identical replacements of
previously viewed exports. SVG/PDF/TSV digests bind delivery files; they do not
assert scientific truth or an independent visual readback of every file format.
No third-party code was executed to claim package/backend acceptance. No analysis
was fitted for these examples. Penguin data are CC0 source measurements; source
row identity does not independently establish repeated-animal identity.

Font checks and geometry checks alone never grant visual acceptance. A changed
dataset, selection, renderer, label, output size or layout needs a new review.
The same styling cannot guarantee beauty or information balance for arbitrary
future data. Review the result at its actual intended use size and repair it.
