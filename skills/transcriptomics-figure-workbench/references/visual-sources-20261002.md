## General layout lesson

Long internal IDs and repeated axis titles can impede comparison. Use concise display aliases with an exact ID mapping, explicit numeric units/scales, annotation lanes and an identical-input comparison. A style diagnosis does not establish biological replication or preservation of an inferred module. The distributed before/after examples use synthetic matrices.
