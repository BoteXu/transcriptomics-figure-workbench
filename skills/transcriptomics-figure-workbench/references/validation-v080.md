## Limits that remain explicit

- Current exports are `SCREEN_REVIEWED_TARGET_SIZE_UNASSESSED`; the nominal 178 mm target has not received calibrated paper or final manuscript-size acceptance.
- Earlier 140 size warnings remain attached to the archived exports. Enlarging a dense overview does not make it legible at every later reduction.
- There was no independent second reviewer, exhaustive native-pixel review or calibrated paper proof. Dense networks and long compositions may still need separate panels at the chosen publication size.
- Screen inspection, checksums and rendering success do not establish scientific validity. Synthetic display examples are not biological findings. Public structural reference images retain their separate provenance.
- `visual_acceptance` remains false until a chosen final physical layout is actually assessed. Source or output changes invalidate a previous bound review.
- Flexible layouts preserve complete panels at uniform scale; they do not silently trim categories, refit a model, recompute enrichment or infer shared axes/legends.

Use the current library browser for exact sources and the archived records for historical scope. Do not carry an old acceptance flag across changed exports.
